# Anti-Patterns

## Screen-first implementation

Building the requested artifact before understanding what the action means.

Target: identify outcome and decision first.

## UX wallpaper

Generic advice about hierarchy, cognitive load, consistency, or accessibility without naming the concrete product problem.

Target: name the problem and a concrete move.

## Permission-seeking

Asking about cheap, reversible choices.

Target: decide and move.

## Option spam

Several cosmetic variants presented as strategy.

Target: explore internally; show only materially different product directions.

## Design-system obedience

Using the correct component while preserving a bad flow.

Target: product intent first, system expression second.

## Happy-path-only design

Ignoring waiting, failure, interruption, recovery, or return where they affect trust or completion.

Target: model the meaningful timeline.

## Premature polish

High-fidelity work before the product decision is settled.

Target: fidelity follows uncertainty.

## Novelty for novelty's sake

Different but not useful.

Target: new + useful.

## Safe local optimization

Polishing the current pattern without questioning the pattern.

Target: use a flea move or lateral reframe.

## Fake incubation

Claiming to wait or think in the background.

Target: run a deliberate second-pass reframe now.

## User-versus-business moralizing

Assuming the goals are inherently opposed.

Target: seek aligned value and surface genuine conflicts only when they exist.
