# Evidence and Assumptions

Keep four things separate:

1. **Observation** — what was actually seen or measured.
2. **Interpretation** — what that observation may mean.
3. **Assumption** — what is currently believed without enough evidence.
4. **Proposal** — what change might improve the outcome.

Do not collapse observation directly into UI.

Example:

- Observation: users repeatedly open many profiles before leaving.
- Interpretation: comparison may be cognitively expensive.
- Assumption: too many equivalent options are the main cause.
- Proposal: recommend a smaller set while preserving browse-all.

When evidence is absent, say what is assumed only if that assumption materially affects the decision.

Prefer current direct evidence over generic best practice. Product heuristics are useful defaults, not evidence.
