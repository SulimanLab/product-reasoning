# Semantics and Usage

The most dangerous translation residue is often semantic: the words look Arabic, but the relationships between them come from another language.

## 1. Diagnose the collocation, not the dictionary word

Do not ask only:

> Is this Arabic word correct?

Ask:

> Is this combination a natural way to express this meaning in Arabic?

## 2. Imported metaphor

Source:
`What does anxiety look like for you?`

❌ `كيف يبدو القلق بالنسبة لك؟`

✅ `كيف يظهر القلق عندك؟`

The source uses visual metaphor for manifestation. Arabic need not preserve it.

## 3. “Behind” metaphors

Source:
`What's behind this feeling?`

Possible literal residue:

❌ `ما الذي يقف خلف هذا الشعور؟`

Depending on intent:

✅ `ما وراء هذا الشعور؟`

✅ `ما الذي قد يسبب هذا الشعور؟`

Use the second when causation is the real meaning.

## 4. “Make progress”

🟡 `إحراز تقدم` is valid formal Arabic and may fit reports.

In personal product copy:

✅ `أنت تتقدم.`

✅ `قطعت شوطًا جيدًا.`

Context decides.

## 5. Self-help and marketing slogans

Do not preserve a slogan merely because each word has an Arabic equivalent.

Examples to inspect:

- `أفضل نسخة من نفسك`;
- `أطلق العنان لإمكاناتك`;
- `ابدأ رحلتك`;
- `خذ لحظة`;
- `على بعد خطوة واحدة`.

Rewrite the concrete promise.

Example:

❌ `أطلق العنان لإمكاناتك.`

If the product teaches a skill:

✅ `طوّر مهاراتك.`

## 6. Modern terms are not guilty by association

Do not automatically replace terms such as:

- `تجربة المستخدم`;
- `منصة`;
- `واجهة`;
- `تطبيق`;
- `يدعم`;
- `بسيط`.

A modern semantic extension may be fully conventional.

Change only when the expression is unnatural or imprecise in context.

## 7. Stable terminology beats stylistic churn

In a product, an established term has value.

If the product consistently uses `الإشعارات`, do not alternate with `التنبيهات` merely for elegance unless the concepts differ.

## 8. Never hide a meaning change behind “naturalness”

Natural rewriting cannot alter:

- legal obligation;
- clinical meaning;
- safety guidance;
- payment terms;
- consent;
- permissions;
- measurable product semantics.

When precision and lightness conflict, preserve precision and flag the trade-off.
