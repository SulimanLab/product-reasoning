# Syntax and Morphology

Use these as **tests**, not a prohibition list.

## 1. Condition versus time

English `when` can express time or an expected condition. Arabic should choose the relation.

Conditional:

🟡 `عندما تواجه مشكلة، تواصل معنا.`

✅ `إذا واجهت مشكلة، تواصل معنا.`

Truly temporal:

🟢 `عندما يكتمل التحميل، سنخبرك.`

Do not replace every `عندما` with `إذا`.

## 2. `في حال`

Often heavier than a native conditional particle.

🟡 `في حال رغبت في المتابعة...`

✅ `إذا أردت المتابعة...`

But keep `الحال` when the noun itself is intended.

## 3. Preposition by meaning

Do not make fixed mappings such as:

- through → من خلال;
- against → ضد;
- toward → تجاه;
- for → لـ;

Rewrite according to the semantic relation.

Example:

❌ `أرسل الطلب من خلال التطبيق.`

✅ `أرسل الطلب من التطبيق.`

or, if the meaning is simply location:

✅ `أرسل الطلب في التطبيق.`

## 4. Purpose

🟡 `من أجل إكمال التسجيل`

✅ `لإكمال التسجيل`

Do not shorten when the longer expression carries real contrast or emphasis.

## 5. Verb before action-shell

❌ `قم بتحديث التطبيق.`

✅ `حدّث التطبيق.`

❌ `يمكنك القيام بتغيير الموعد.`

✅ `يمكنك تغيير الموعد.`

## 6. Attached pronouns

❌ `الحساب الخاص بك`

✅ `حسابك`

Use the longer form only when it resolves ambiguity or contrast.

## 7. Prefer the event over the abstract process noun

❌ `إتمام عملية الدفع`

✅ `إتمام الدفع`

❌ `بدء عملية التسجيل`

✅ `بدء التسجيل`

## 8. Comparative morphology

Where natural:

🟡 `الخيار الأكثر وضوحًا`

✅ `الخيار الأوضح`

But do not apply mechanically. Some adjectives do not form a usable direct comparative in the intended sense, and modern usage may favor the analytic form.

## 9. Causative/derived forms

Arabic morphology can sometimes carry causation directly.

However, **availability is not enough**. Use a derived form only when it is current and natural.

The purpose of this rule is to notice Arabic's capacity, not to produce rare forms.

## 10. Future marking

Do not add `سـ` or `سوف` merely because English has `will` if sequence or context already makes futurity obvious.

Keep them when future reference needs to be explicit.

## 11. Restriction / exclusivity

Do not assume `only` must always become `فقط`.

Test native structures such as `لا ... إلا` or `إنما` when they are natural.

But `فقط` is common modern Arabic and is not automatically wrong.

## 12. Existential structure

Do not automatically copy `there is / there are`.

❌ `هناك تحديث متاح.`

Often lighter:

✅ `يتوفر تحديث.`

or

✅ `تحديث متاح.`

Yet `هناك` itself remains valid when it genuinely serves the sentence.
