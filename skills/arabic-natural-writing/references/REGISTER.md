# Register

## Default

**فصحى معاصرة طبيعية، قريبة من الكلام دون أن تصبح عامية.**

Natural Arabic is not necessarily colloquial, and formal Arabic is not necessarily better Arabic.

## 1. Formal MSA

Use for legal, compliance, contractual, and high-consequence institutional text.

Example:

`يحق لك إلغاء الاشتراك خلال 14 يومًا.`

## 2. Contemporary natural MSA — default

Example:

`يمكنك إلغاء الاشتراك في أي وقت.`

## 3. Local warmth

A Saudi/Gulf-facing brand may use lighter local phrasing intentionally.

Do not sprinkle dialect words randomly.

Bad register mixing:

`يرجى إدخال بريدك الإلكتروني، وبعدها يلا نكمل.`

Choose one coherent voice.

## 4. Dialect

Use only when product voice explicitly calls for it.

Nationality alone is not enough to infer dialect.

## 5. Do not “correct” familiar local nouns into bureaucracy

Saudi consumer context:

🟢 `رقم الجوال`

Do not replace it with `رقم الهاتف المحمول` solely to sound formal.

## 6. Sensitive contexts

In health, mental health, finance, grief, crisis, privacy, or safety:

- reduce playfulness;
- avoid marketing clichés;
- avoid forced optimism;
- avoid over-familiarity;
- keep instructions precise.

Natural does not mean casual.
