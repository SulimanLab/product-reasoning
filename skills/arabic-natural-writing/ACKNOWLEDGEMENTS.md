# Acknowledgements

This project owes its main conceptual framing to أحمد الغامدي's work on **العَرَنجية**, especially the distinction between Arabic-looking sentences and Arabic-native expression.

It also benefited from `AbadLife/ux-araby` as a practical example of packaging Arabic UX guidance into an Agent Skill.

The resulting method adds two explicit operating rules for modern product writing:

1. **Rebuild from meaning rather than repairing literal translation.**
2. **Treat suspicious patterns as diagnostics, not automatic prohibitions.**

The project is independently authored and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the book author or the maintainers of `ux-araby`.
